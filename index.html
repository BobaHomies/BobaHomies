Our Story

Welcome to Boba Homies, your cozy corner for artisanal milk teas and house-made toppings. Inspired by the comfort of a café and the joy of bubble tea, we craft every drink with high-quality tea leaves, creamy milk, and freshly cooked tapioca pearls. Whether you're here for a quick treat or to unwind, we hope you’ll feel right at home with every sip.
